about me

Hi, I’m Tobias, a photographer based in the Netherlands.

This portfolio is a place for me to document my progress, experiment with different styles, and share the photographs I make along the way. I’m especially interested in film photography and capturing ordinary places, landscape, people, a little of everything.

I’m still developing my own style, so this website will continue to change and grow together with my photography.

I’m open to photography assignments and collaborations. Feel free to get in touch with any questions or if you’re interested in working together.
